Two hunters, one duck

Duck Shooter

A duck cuts across the marsh. The first shot that hits it scores.

  • Player 1 aims with the mouse and clicks to shoot.
  • Player 2 aims with the arrow keys or WASD, and shoots with Space.
  • Each hunter gets 3 shots per duck. The earlier hit wins.
  • Wheel, pinch, or the + and − buttons zoom a scope. Right-click toggles it. The keyboard hunter uses Q and E.
  • Esc leaves the marsh. M mutes the guns.
